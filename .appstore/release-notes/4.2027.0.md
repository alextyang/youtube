# ImprovedTube 4.2027.0

Draft for App Store Connect build `1332`. This update replaces the unreleased
4.1322.0 (build 1331), so the text covers every change since the last published
version, 4.1321.0. Feature claims are backed by reviewed Safari results; a few
small upstream fixes the inventory does not cover are marked as such. The
mapping, plus everything left out and why, is in [4.2027.0-audit.md](4.2027.0-audit.md).

## App Store update text

Smart Speed, rebuilt:

- Smart Speed has a new engine that adapts playback speed within the minimum and maximum you set, with an on-screen speed indicator.
- It can use SponsorBlock community segments to speed through sponsor, intro, and outro sections, and can show Skip Intro and Skip Sponsor buttons.
- New options for videos without heatmap data, a threshold for short videos, and separate handling for Shorts and live streams.

Playback speed and player:

- The playback speed buttons are combined into one button that remembers your custom speed.
- Adjust playback speed in custom steps, including with the scroll wheel.
- New option to block the Opus audio codec.
- Cinema mode now applies after a page refresh, and the repeat button appears even when YouTube loads the player controls late.
- Forced playback speed now recognizes music videos more reliably.

Also new and improved:

- Collapsible side-panel sections.
- Better handling of automatically dubbed audio while keeping human-made dubbed tracks.
- Live chat no longer leaves an empty column when collapsed.
- Azerbaijani translation, more localized menu labels, and refined Russian, Japanese, and Urdu translations.

Fixes:

- Subtitles turn on reliably when a video first loads.
- Keyboard shortcuts recover after a missed key release.
- Fixed the Subscriptions list layout, Add to Blocklist visibility in dark mode, and square-corner styling for Shorts thumbnails and previews.
- Comment actions stay available when author avatars are hidden.
- Player tooltips stay inside the window and above the video.
- Context-menu actions no longer run twice.
- Exported settings files include a timestamp in their name.
- Safer Safari App Store packaging and validation.
