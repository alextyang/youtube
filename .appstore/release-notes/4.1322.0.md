# ImprovedTube 4.1322.0

## App Store update text

Safari improvements come first in this update:

- Improved Safari reliability when YouTube loads or replaces player elements, including more dependable player controls, Fit-to-Window, subtitles, and recovery features.
- Exported settings now use timestamped filenames, including Safari's download fallback.
- Strengthened Safari App Store packaging and validation for safer updates.

New and improved:

- Added an optional Volume Boost player button with 1x, 2x, and 3x levels.
- Added collapsible side-panel accordions and an option to hide the “Top live games” home-page section.
- Smart Speed now uses the configured minimum speed when YouTube has no heatmap data.
- Improved auto video recovery, auto-continue watching, and handling of automatically dubbed audio while preserving human-made dubbed tracks.
- Refined Russian and Japanese translations.

Fixes:

- Restored keyboard shortcuts after missed key-release events and prevented videos from unexpectedly starting across multiple tabs.
- Fixed Subscriptions list layout, dark-mode visibility of the Add to Blocklist button, and square-thumbnail styling for Shorts and previews.
- Fixed playlist Quick Delete, Watch Later confirmation, subtitles on initial load, context-menu duplicate actions, and audio-menu layout after navigation.
- Kept player tooltips inside the viewport and above the video.
- Restored comment actions when author avatars are hidden.
- Removed duplicate playback-speed controls and settings.
